Good reminder emails are brief, specific, and easy to act on. They do not need legal language to be effective.

## Template 1: Friendly first reminder

Hi [Customer Name],

Just a quick note that invoice [Invoice Number] for [Amount] was due on [Due Date]. If payment has already been made, please ignore this email. If not, you can complete payment here: [Payment Link].

Thank you,
[Your Name]

## Template 2: Firm second reminder

Hi [Customer Name],

Invoice [Invoice Number] for [Amount] remains unpaid. Please arrange payment as soon as possible, or reply if there is an issue we should be aware of.

Payment link: [Payment Link]

Regards,
[Your Name]

## Template 3: Final overdue notice

Hi [Customer Name],

This is a final reminder that invoice [Invoice Number] for [Amount] is now seriously overdue. Please arrange payment immediately or contact us today if the invoice is in dispute.

Payment link: [Payment Link]

Regards,
[Your Name]

## How to use the templates well

- Keep the factual details consistent across all stages.
- Change the tone gradually instead of jumping from friendly to hostile.
- Offer a reply path for disputes or payment timing issues.
- Stop the sequence once the customer has promised to pay or raised a dispute.
