Once you've connected an invoice source, PaidSoon automatically imports your outstanding invoices
so they can enter your reminder sequence.

## Before you start

Connect at least one invoice source first:

- [Connect PaidSoon to Xero](/help/connect-xero)
- [Connect PaidSoon to MYOB Business](/help/connect-myob)
- Or connect Stripe directly via **Settings → Connections**

## Steps

1. After connecting a source, PaidSoon runs a first import automatically — you don't need to do
   anything.
2. Go to **Settings → Connections** to check progress. Each connection card shows one of:
   - **Importing…** — the first import is still running
   - **Active** — the first import completed and invoices are syncing normally
   - **Sync error** — the last import attempt failed
3. To trigger an import manually at any time, click **Sync now** on the connection card
   (**Retry sync** if the connection is in an error state).

## What you'll see

While an import is running, the connection card shows **Importing…**. Once it finishes, the card
updates to **Active** and shows the timestamp of the last successful sync. A short history of
recent sync runs (start time, invoices created/updated, and any error) is also shown on the card.

## What happens next

Imported invoices appear in your dashboard's invoice table with a **Pending** status and become
eligible for your configured [reminder schedule](/help/configure-reminder-schedule). PaidSoon
keeps re-syncing periodically after the first import to pick up new invoices and payment updates.

<Callout title="Invoice missing after import?">
  PaidSoon only imports invoices that match your source's overdue criteria at the time of sync.
  If an invoice becomes overdue after the last sync, it will appear on the next sync — click
  **Sync now** to check immediately rather than waiting.
</Callout>

## Common problems

- **Import seems stuck on "Importing…"** — click **Sync now** to retry; large invoice histories
  can take longer on the very first import.
- **"Sync error" keeps appearing** — click **Retry sync**; if it persists, disconnect and
  reconnect the source (see the Xero or MYOB tutorials above).
