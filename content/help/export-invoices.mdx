Invoice export lets you download your invoice data as a CSV or XLSX file for use in a spreadsheet
or accounting tool. It's available on the **Small Business** plan and above.

## Quick export from the dashboard

1. Go to your **Invoices** dashboard.
2. Click **Export CSV** or **Export XLSX** in the toolbar next to **Import invoices**.
3. The file downloads with the invoices currently shown (matching the active status/overview-card
   filter).

## Advanced export from Settings

1. Go to **Settings → Invoice exports**.
2. Choose a format (CSV or XLSX).
3. Optionally filter by status, customer, or accounting source, or turn on **Filter by date
   range** to filter by due date or created date.
4. Click **Generate export** to download the file.

If no invoices match your filters, you'll see a message instead of an empty file.

## What's in the file

Each row includes the invoice reference, customer name and email, due date, original amount,
outstanding balance, currency, status, paid date, promise-to-pay status/date, dispute status,
reminder stage, accounting source, and created/updated timestamps. Invoice date is only populated
for invoices imported from a spreadsheet.

## A note on the XLSX file

The XLSX file includes column filters (autofilter) and sensible column widths. The header row is
not frozen — this is a known limitation of the spreadsheet library PaidSoon uses, not a bug.
