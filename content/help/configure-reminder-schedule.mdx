PaidSoon sends up to three follow-up emails per invoice, spaced out after the due date. This
tutorial shows you where to set the timing.

## Before you start

You need at least one connected invoice source (see
[connect Xero](/help/connect-xero) or [connect MYOB Business](/help/connect-myob)) so there are
invoices for the schedule to apply to.

## Steps

1. Go to **Settings → Schedule** (`/dashboard/settings/schedule`).
2. You'll see three fields, one per reminder stage:
   - **Email 1 — days after due date**
   - **Email 2 — days after due date**
   - **Email 3 — days after due date**
3. Enter how many days after the due date each email should send (for example, 3, 10, and 21).
4. Click **Save schedule**.

## What you'll see

After saving, a **Saved!** confirmation appears briefly under the form.

<Callout title="On the Starter plan?">
  Starter accounts use a fixed default schedule (day 3, day 10, day 21) and the fields are
  read-only, with a message noting that custom timing is available on higher plans. Every plan
  still gets the full three-stage reminder sequence — only the ability to change the timing is
  plan-gated.
</Callout>

## What happens next

New and existing overdue invoices are scheduled against the saved timing. Changing the schedule
does not resend emails that have already gone out — it only affects when future reminders send.

## Common problems

- **Fields are greyed out and won't save** — this means custom timing isn't available on your
  current plan; the schedule still runs on the default 3/10/21-day timing.
- **An invoice didn't get a reminder on the expected day** — check whether it's been
  [paused](/help/pause-reminders) or has an active
  [promise to pay](/help/record-a-promise-to-pay), both of which suspend the schedule for that
  invoice.
