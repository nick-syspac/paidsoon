If a client paid you directly (bank transfer, cash, or anything your invoice source won't
automatically detect), you can manually resolve the invoice so PaidSoon stops chasing it.

## Before you start

Only resolve an invoice once you're confident it's genuinely settled — manually resolving does
not record a payment in your accounting software or Stripe; it only stops PaidSoon's reminders.

## Steps

1. Go to your dashboard's invoice table (`/dashboard`).
2. Select the invoice (or invoices) you want to resolve using the checkboxes.
3. Click **Resolve** in the bulk action bar.
4. Click **Confirm resolve** to finish.

## What you'll see

The invoice's status badge changes to **Resolved**, and it moves out of your active invoice list
into **Resolved Invoices** (`/dashboard/resolved`), alongside invoices your source reports as
paid.

## What happens next

Resolved invoices no longer receive reminder emails and are excluded from your active overdue
totals. They still count toward your all-time collection-rate metric on the dashboard.

<Callout title="This can't be undone from the dashboard">
  There's currently no "un-resolve" action — if you resolve an invoice by mistake, contact
  support@paidsoon.com.au.
</Callout>

## Common problems

- **Invoice reappears as unpaid after resolving** — if your invoice source re-syncs and still
  reports the invoice as outstanding, check with your accounting software directly; PaidSoon
  reflects what your source reports.
- **Accidentally resolved the wrong invoice** — contact support@paidsoon.com.au for help.
