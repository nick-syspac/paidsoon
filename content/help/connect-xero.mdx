Connect your Xero organisation so PaidSoon can automatically import overdue invoices and start
sending reminder sequences on your behalf.

## Before you start

- You need to be signed in to PaidSoon and have admin access to the Xero organisation you want to
  connect.
- Each PaidSoon account has a limit on how many invoice sources (Stripe and accounting
  connections combined) it can connect at once — check your plan's connection limit under
  **Settings → Connections** if you already have a connection set up.

## Steps

1. Go to **Settings → Connections** (`/dashboard/settings/connections`).
2. Under **Accounting Integrations**, find the Xero card and click **Connect Xero**.
3. You'll be redirected to Xero to sign in and authorise PaidSoon.
4. If your Xero login has access to more than one organisation, Xero will ask you to pick one on
   PaidSoon's organisation-selection screen before continuing.
5. Once authorised, PaidSoon redirects you back to **Settings → Connections**.

## What you'll see

Right after connecting, the Xero card shows a status of **Importing…** while PaidSoon pulls in
your overdue invoices for the first time. This can take a little while for organisations with a
lot of invoice history.

<Callout title="First sync still running?">
  If the card still says **Importing…** after a minute or two, click **Sync now** on the card to
  retry. If it shows **Sync error**, click **Retry sync** — if it keeps failing, contact
  support@paidsoon.com.au.
</Callout>

## What happens next

Once the status badge changes to **Active**, your overdue Xero invoices appear in your PaidSoon
dashboard and are eligible for automated reminder sequences based on your
[reminder schedule](/help/configure-reminder-schedule). PaidSoon keeps syncing periodically after
the first import, and you can trigger a manual **Sync now** at any time from the connection card.

## Common problems

- **"No organisations found in your account"** — make sure the Xero user you signed in with has
  access to at least one organisation.
- **Connection stuck on "Sync error"** — click **Retry sync**. If it fails repeatedly, disconnect
  and reconnect Xero.
- **Reached your connection limit** — disconnect an unused source under **Settings →
  Connections**, or upgrade your plan for a higher connection limit.
