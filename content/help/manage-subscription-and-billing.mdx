Use subscription settings to manage your current plan, billing status, and renewal behavior.

## Before you start

- Sign in to PaidSoon and open **Settings → Subscription** (`/dashboard/settings/subscription`).
- Have the billing owner role for your account so you can confirm plan changes.

## Steps

1. Review your **Current plan** card and billing status.
2. Select another plan to start a change:
   - **Upgrade** opens Stripe checkout to apply the change immediately.
   - **Downgrade** schedules the change for your next renewal date.
3. Use **Manage billing** to open the Stripe customer portal for card and invoice administration.
4. If needed, choose **Cancel subscription** (or **End free trial**) and confirm on the cancellation flow.

## What you'll see

- Success messages after completed changes.
- Scheduled downgrade notices showing the effective date.
- Current status labels such as active, trialing, past due, or cancelled.

## What happens next

- Upgrades take effect immediately after successful Stripe checkout.
- Downgrades apply at the next billing boundary unless cancelled before that date.
- Cancellation schedules end-of-term access unless your flow states immediate trial end.

## Common problems

- **I cannot see upgrade options**: Confirm you are on the subscription page while signed in.
- **Manage billing does not open**: Disable popup blockers and try again.
- **Plan did not change yet**: Scheduled downgrades apply at renewal, not immediately.
