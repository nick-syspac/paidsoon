Connecting Stripe lets PaidSoon detect overdue invoices and include Stripe-hosted payment links in reminder emails.

## Before you start

- You need a Stripe account with permission to authorise connections.
- You must be signed in to PaidSoon.
- Each plan has a limit on connected invoice sources (Stripe plus accounting connections combined).

## Steps

1. Go to **Settings → Connections** (`/dashboard/settings/connections`).
2. In the **Stripe** card, click **Connect Stripe**.
3. Complete the Stripe OAuth flow and approve access.
4. You are returned to **Settings → Connections** after authorisation.

## What you'll see

- A success banner appears on return.
- The Stripe card shows the account as connected.
- PaidSoon starts importing overdue invoices for that connected source.

## What happens next

- Imported overdue invoices appear in your dashboard and enter your reminder workflow.
- Reminder emails for Stripe-sourced invoices include a direct payment link when Stripe provides one.
- You can disconnect Stripe later from the same settings page.

## Common problems

- **Connection was cancelled**: Start the flow again and complete Stripe authorisation.
- **Connection limit reached**: Disconnect an unused source or upgrade your plan.
- **No invoices appear yet**: Wait for the initial import, then refresh and review your dashboard.
