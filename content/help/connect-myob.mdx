Connect your MYOB Business company file so PaidSoon can import overdue invoices and start sending
reminder sequences.

<Callout title="Early access">
  MYOB Business support is available to early-access users while PaidSoon finishes validating
  full production support. If you hit an issue, please let us know at
  support@paidsoon.com.au — early feedback directly shapes what ships next.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- You need to be signed in to PaidSoon and have access to the MYOB Business company file you want
  to connect.
- Unlike Xero, MYOB doesn't offer a company-file picker inside PaidSoon — MYOB's own sign-in
  screen determines which company file you're authorising, so make sure you sign in to MYOB with
  the account tied to the right company file.

## Steps

1. Go to **Settings → Connections** (`/dashboard/settings/connections`).
2. Under **Accounting Integrations**, find the MYOB Business card and click **Connect MYOB
   Business**.
3. Sign in to MYOB and authorise PaidSoon when prompted.
4. MYOB redirects you back to **Settings → Connections** automatically — there's no separate
   company-file selection step inside PaidSoon.

## What you'll see

The MYOB Business card shows **Importing…** while PaidSoon completes its first invoice import.
MYOB connections can occasionally take slightly longer to become active right after authorising,
since MYOB's own systems need a short moment to fully activate a new connection.

<Callout title="Still importing after a couple of minutes?">
  Click **Sync now** on the card to retry manually. If the card shows **Sync error**, click
  **Retry sync** — if it keeps failing, contact support@paidsoon.com.au.
</Callout>

## What happens next

Once the card shows **Active**, your overdue MYOB Business invoices appear in your PaidSoon
dashboard and become eligible for your configured
[reminder schedule](/help/configure-reminder-schedule).

## Common problems

- **Connection authorised but no invoices appear** — click **Sync now**; first-sync timing can
  vary slightly for MYOB.
- **Repeated "Sync error"** — disconnect and reconnect. Since this is an early-access
  integration, please report persistent errors to support@paidsoon.com.au with your business
  name so we can investigate.
