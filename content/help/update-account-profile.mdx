Your account settings let you keep profile and account details current without affecting invoice data.

## Before you start

- Sign in to PaidSoon.
- Open **Settings → Account** (`/dashboard/settings/account`).

## Steps

1. In the **Profile** section, enter your preferred **Display name**.
2. Click **Save**.
3. Review account details shown on the page:
   - current plan
   - subscription status
   - member-since date
4. Use the settings navigation to move to related pages such as **Connections**, **Subscription**, and **Import / Export**.

## What you'll see

- A success message when the display name is saved.
- Your account email shown as read-only.
- Plan and status values that match your current subscription record.

## What happens next

- The display name token in reminder templates (`{{yourName}}`) uses your saved value.
- Other settings sections stay unchanged until you edit them directly.

## Common problems

- **Save button is disabled**: Enter a non-empty display name first.
- **Save failed**: Retry and check your network connection.
- **Email cannot be edited here**: This page is for profile display name and account summary only.
