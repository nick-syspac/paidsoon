## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The page SHALL target accountant and bookkeeper search intent with current-value positioning
`/accountants` SHALL target both accountants and bookkeepers in its visible headings and metadata, and SHALL lead with the value that is available today for managing invoice follow-up on behalf of individual clients before presenting planned multi-client capabilities.

#### Scenario: Visitor arrives from an accountant-focused search
- **WHEN** a visitor views `/accountants`
- **THEN** the page title, heading, and introductory copy refer to accountants and bookkeepers
- **AND** the page frames PaidSoon as invoice reminder or debtor-follow-up support rather than only a generic partner programme

#### Scenario: Current value precedes planned partner tooling
- **WHEN** a visitor scans the page from top to bottom
- **THEN** the first substantive value section describes currently available single-client workflows
- **AND** planned multi-client management remains clearly secondary and labeled as not yet available
